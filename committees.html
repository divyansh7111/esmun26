Committees & Agendas

Explore the committees and agendas that will shape the discussions of ESMUN'26.

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

Agenda

Deliberating on the escalation of tensions between Iran and Gulf States, with emphasis on the Strait of Hormuz, proxy conflicts, and emerging non-traditional warfare.

AIPPM

All India Political Parties Meet

Agenda

Deliberation on systemic reforms in India's education framework, with special emphasis on examination integrity, equitable access to quality education, and the growing influence of shadow education, while examining the state's responsibility under the Constitutional Golden Triangle of Articles 14, 19, and 21.

CCC

Continuous Crisis Committee

Agenda

Assessing state stability and political transition in Egypt during the 2011 uprising, with focus on regime change, civil unrest, and military intervention.

Freeze Date: 21 February 2011- Aftermath of Hosni Mubarak’s resignation

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

Agenda

Examining human rights violations in the escalating West Asian conflict, with emphasis on the role of major state and non-state actors.

UNODC

United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime

Agenda

Addressing the challenges posed by encrypted digital platforms while safeguarding fundamental rights in combating transnational organized crime.

UNCSW

United Nations Commission on the Status of Women

Agenda

Combating misogyny and the objectification of women and girls through digital governance, with special focus on women’s education and awareness in online ecosystems.

IP

International Press

Theme

Navigating narratives in the age of misinformation: the role of media in shaping perception, accountability, and truth in global discourse.
