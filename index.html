About ESMUN'26

ESMUN'26 brings together aspiring diplomats, critical thinkers, and future leaders to engage in meaningful dialogue on the challenges shaping our world. Through debate, collaboration, and negotiation, delegates explore innovative pathways towards a better future.

Recoding the Future

The future is not something we inherit unchanged; it is something we actively redesign. ESMUN'26 challenges delegates to rethink systems, institutions, and global frameworks to create solutions that are adaptive, inclusive, and forward-looking.
