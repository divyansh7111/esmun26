About ESMUN'26

A platform where diplomacy, leadership, and global dialogue converge to shape the leaders of tomorrow.

Founded with the vision of fostering diplomacy, leadership, and informed global discourse, EuroSchool Model United Nations (ESMUN) has grown into a dynamic platform for young changemakers and aspiring global leaders.

Hosted by EuroSchool, Thane, ESMUN brings together students from diverse backgrounds to engage with pressing international issues through debate, negotiation, and collaboration. What began as an ambitious initiative has steadily evolved into a thriving conference, welcoming an increasing number of delegates and schools with each edition.

Following the success of our previous conferences, which brought together over 200 delegates from more than 30 schools, ESMUN returns for its Third Edition with an even greater commitment to excellence, innovation, and meaningful discourse.

At ESMUN, delegates step into the roles of diplomats and decision-makers, developing essential skills in public speaking, research, critical thinking, negotiation, and leadership. Beyond committee sessions, the conference serves as a space where ideas are exchanged, perspectives are challenged, and lasting connections are formed.

ESMUN'26 is more than a conference—it is a growing legacy of diplomacy, ambition, and youth leadership.
